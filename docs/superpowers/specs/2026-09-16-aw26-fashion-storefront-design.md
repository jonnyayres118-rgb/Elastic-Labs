# ELASTIC LABS AW26 Storefront Design

## Purpose
Transform the existing Elastic Labs site into a black, high-fashion made-to-order house for AW26.

## Positioning
HUMAN FORM, EXPANDED. Sculptural armour for a moving world. The customer-facing vocabulary is protection, movement, human architecture, modularity and armour. The collection is black, made to order and individually numbered.

## Experience
The storefront should feel like entering a fashion house rather than a conventional ecommerce catalogue: black canvas, sparse typography, huge statements, severe grid, high negative space and minimal conventional UI. Core sections: AW26 hero, manifesto, collection, made-to-order process, archive and contact.

## Commerce boundary
V1 is a functioning catalogue and enquiry/claim experience. Shopify checkout, inventory/order APIs and factory workflow are intentionally separate integration work because no Shopify store connection is currently available in this session. Do not represent email claims as completed purchases.

## Production model
Nothing is produced before order. Every finished object receives a unique serial and provenance record. Consumer terms, sizing, manufacturing lead time, returns/cancellation rights, duties and quality-control process must be finalised before accepting live payments.
