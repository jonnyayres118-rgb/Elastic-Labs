# ELASTIC LABS AW26 Instagram Launch System

Date: 17 September 2026  
Status: Approved design  
Campaign: THE LOCKED ARCHIVE

## 1. Objective

Launch ELASTIC LABS as a collectible fashion house rather than a conventional clothing label. The campaign must make the seven AW26 originals desirable before broad access exists, convert serious interest into private conversations, and direct wider demand into THE LEDGER waitlist for later numbered diffusion editions.

The emotional sequence is: intrigue → frustration → private access → ownership.

## 2. Positioning

**Master statement:** HUMAN FORM, EXPANDED.

**Campaign statement:** Seven originals exist. Most people will never own one. A limited number will be allowed into what follows.

ELASTIC LABS creates sculptural armour for a moving world. The pieces are wearable objects: functional, confrontational, numbered and recorded. Scarcity is factual. The brand must never use fake countdowns, fake sell-outs or invented demand.

## 3. Access Model

The campaign combines three access modes:

1. **Public denial:** The feed shows the work while stating that public access is closed.
2. **Private claim:** Serious prospects may DM `OBJECT + object number` to discuss an original.
3. **The Ledger:** The bio link collects people who want first access to numbered diffusion editions.

Public CTA language:
- PUBLIC ACCESS: CLOSED.
- DM OBJECT + [NUMBER].
- ENTER THE LEDGER FOR WHAT FOLLOWS.

The main website remains an editorial collection experience until ordering, sizing, production, terms and fulfilment are operationally ready.

## 4. Ownership Hierarchy

| Level | Edition | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| ORIGINAL | 1/1 | Unique AW26 object; never reproduced in precisely the same execution |
| EDITION I | 1/10 | Closest numbered interpretation of the original |
| EDITION II | 1/25 | Rare diffusion object |
| EDITION III | 1/50 | Limited seasonal object |
| EDITION IV | 1/100 | Entry object into the ELASTIC LABS archive |

Each physical piece carries:
- ELASTIC LABS wordmark
- collection season
- object code
- individual edition number, e.g. `07/25`
- permanent archive record

The numbering belongs to the object, not merely its packaging. The object becomes the art.

## 5. Visual System

### Identity

Use the current ELASTIC LABS uppercase wordmark as the launch identity. Do not introduce a separate symbol at this stage. Typography is severe, geometric and widely tracked. The wordmark must remain readable, restrained and consistent.

### Palette

- Carbon black
- Soft black
- Graphite
- Dirty ecru
- Cold silver
- Extremely limited warning white

No bright fashion colour is introduced during the original-object launch.

### Image Language

The campaign combines:
- full-body brutalist fashion editorials
- isolated product portraits
- macro studies of stitching, fastenings, padding and serial markings
- severe architectural environments
- negative space
- controlled motion blur
- harsh directional lighting and deep blacks

The work should feel expensive, strange, protective and human. It must not look like generic tactical cosplay, inexpensive techwear, a gaming character, a military uniform or a mass-market trainer campaign.

### Instagram Formats

- Feed portrait: 1080 × 1350, 4:5
- Carousel slides: 1080 × 1350, 4:5
- Story/Reel covers: 1080 × 1920, 9:16
- Profile grid safe area: essential subject and type remain within the central square
- Master generation: high-resolution portrait source suitable for both feed and Story crops

Text is added as a consistent graphic layer after imagery is approved. Image generation should prioritise product fidelity and clean composition rather than attempting dense generated typography.

## 8. Carousel Structure Per SKU

1. **Reveal:** Full object and name.
2. **Architecture:** Detail image with one construction statement.
3. **Identity:** SKU, season, material direction and `ORIGINAL / 1 OF 1`.
4. **Access:** `PUBLIC ACCESS: CLOSED` plus `DM OBJECT [NUMBER]`.

Example construction language:
- BUILT AROUND THE BODY. NOT CUT TO FOLLOW IT.
- PROTECTION WITHOUT PERMISSION.
- SOFT MATERIAL. HARD PRESENCE.
- MADE AFTER IT IS CLAIMED.
- THE SERIAL BELONGS TO THE OBJECT.

### Voice Rules

- Short, declarative sentences
- No breathless luxury clichés
- No emojis during the original-object launch
- No discount language
- No false urgency
- No apologising for price or scarcity
- Provocative without insulting the audience

## 10. Hashtag System

Use 6–10 tags per post. Rotate sets rather than repeating a large block.

**Brand set:**  
`#ElasticLabs #HumanFormExpanded #TheLockedArchive #ElasticLabsAW26`

**Fashion set:**  
`#WearableArt #ConceptualFashion #AvantGardeFashion #FutureFashion #FashionObjects #SculpturalFashion`

**Product set:**  
`#MadeToOrder #OneOfOne #LimitedEditionFashion #NumberedEdition #CollectibleDesign #IndependentFashion`

## 12. Production Workflow

1. Rebuild seven product masters at high resolution from the approved AW26 visual language.
2. Review product fidelity and distinguishability before making derivative crops.
3. Produce isolated portraits, detail studies and Story crops for each approved master.
4. Build the four-slide carousel system.
5. Add deterministic wordmark, object number, SKU and edition typography outside the generative image process.
6. Export final feed and Story assets.
7. Package captions, posting order, hashtags and asset filenames in one manifest.
8. Add approved final product masters to the relevant website SKUs.

Asset filename pattern:
- `EL-AW26-[OBJECT]-IG-REVEAL-01.webp`
- `EL-AW26-[OBJECT]-IG-DETAIL-01.webp`
- `EL-AW26-[OBJECT]-IG-DETAIL-02.webp`
- `EL-AW26-[OBJECT]-IG-STORY-01.webp`

## 13. Quality Gates

Before release:
- all seven objects are visually distinct
- each image maps to the correct SKU
- product form remains consistent across a carousel
- no accidental logos, text, watermarks, extra limbs or broken footwear
- black garments retain readable material detail
- wordmark spelling is exact
- object numbers and edition numbers are internally consistent
- central-square crop remains usable on the Instagram grid
- all 4:5 and 9:16 exports meet target dimensions
- CTA matches campaign stage
- no diffusion object is advertised before its design is approved

## 14. Launch Cadence

Recommended initial cadence:
- Day 1: posts 1–3 as a triptych
- Days 2–8: one object per day, led by reveal carousel; supporting detail/ownership posts may follow later the same day or roll into the next
- Day 9: original ownership statement
- Day 10: originals-will-not-return statement
- Day 11: numbered-edition hierarchy
- Day 12: THE LEDGER invitation

The first twelve days prioritise coherence over volume. Stories run daily and direct serious enquiries into DMs while the bio retains THE LEDGER link.

## 15. Success Criteria

The campaign succeeds when:
- the Instagram grid looks like an established fashion house on launch day
- each original has an immediately recognisable visual identity
- enquiries arrive using object numbers rather than vague product descriptions
- THE LEDGER captures demand that cannot access originals
- the audience understands that future products are numbered editions, not unlimited stock
- the visual system can extend to later diffusion releases without redesign
